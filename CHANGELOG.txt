# Changelog

All notable changes to nas-mail-client are documented in this file.

## [0.1.3]

### Changed

- The texts of the interface, in Italian and English, are impersonal and shorter: no ellipsis,
  colon or semicolon, no names of internal programs, commands or variables.
- The scrollbars come from the shared library, which shows only their thumb: no arrows and no
  track.
- The first configuration has only its title and a shorter description; the explanation of the
  server and the hint under it are gone, here and in the settings. Each field of the server has a
  `name`, for the browser to fill it in.

### Removed

- The unused icons `update.svg`, `access.svg`, `system.svg` and the unused text `folders.subject`.

## [0.1.2]

### Added

- A mail when the automatic check of the launcher finds new versions of the installed applications
  (`appUpdatesAvailable`), with its own switch. The mails of that kind are filed as `apps`: the
  `CHECK` of the `mails` table changed, so an existing database does not open: install from
  scratch.

### Changed

- The files of `assets/`, with a hash of their content in the name, are served with
  `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable`, the page with `no-cache`: the browser keeps
  the scripts and the styles between visits instead of asking for them again through the gateway
  and the launcher.
- Without an account that can send, the application shows only its first configuration. Then the
  aside has the folders and, at the bottom, the settings: a modal with the account and the
  notifications, each automatic mail with the switch of the NAS applications.
- The icons are masks in the colour of their text, so they follow the theme.
- The manifest files the application under the `system` category.
- The launcher bridge, the shared styles, the layout with its sidebar, icons, switches, the
  confirmation dialog, the empty folder, the native dialog under `CommonModal` and the build,
  package and release scripts come from the shared library `@valeriogc/nas-base-app`.

## [0.1.1]

### Added

- With an account configured and at least one notification on, the application tells the launcher
  it works without its window (`run.background`): it starts with the NAS and stays on when its
  window closes, at a lower priority. Without, it stops with its window as before.

### Changed

- The tables are `STRICT`, and the index of the folders follows the order the sent mails are read
  in. An existing database does not open: install from scratch.
- In the frame of the launcher the application keeps the rules of its old sandbox by itself
  (`src/services/frameGuard.ts`, called first in `main.ts`): no window of its own, no
  `alert`/`confirm`/`prompt` dialog, no navigation of the page that holds it. The launcher dropped
  the sandbox, which on its origin was no wall and made the browser warn: nothing else changes.

## [0.1.0]

### Added

- SMTP configuration through msmtp, installed on the NAS with the application.
- Mails for refused sign-ins, launcher updates and system updates, each chosen in its own section.
- Sent mail and trash.
